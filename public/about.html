About Betty Car

Your trusted partner for safe, comfortable, and memorable journeys since 2018.

Our Story

Betty Car started with a simple idea: to provide reliable and affordable transportation for the Bishoftu community and beyond. Over the years, we've grown into a trusted name, known for our friendly service, well-maintained vehicles, and commitment to customer satisfaction.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To deliver top-quality car rental experiences with honesty, transparency, and a personal touch.

Vision: To become Ethiopia's most loved car rental brand, setting the standard for service and value.

Our Values

  • Customer First — We go the extra mile for your comfort and safety.
  • Integrity — Honest pricing and transparent service, always.
  • Reliability — Well-maintained cars, ready when you need them.
  • Community — Proudly serving Bishoftu and supporting local growth.

Our Journey

2018: Betty Car founded in Bishoftu with 3 cars.
2020: Expanded fleet to 15 vehicles with online booking.
2022: Served 1,000th customer, won Bishoftu Service Award.
2024: 10+ car types serving all of Oromia region.
2026: Launched full-stack platform with online booking & accounts.
